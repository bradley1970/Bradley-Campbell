# SignalFoundry launch script

## 60–75 second voiceover

Everyone is being told to “start an AI business.” The problem? Most advice gives you fifty ideas and no way to choose.

There are three practical models worth understanding.

First: niche content and SEO. You build the most useful resource for a specific audience, use AI to accelerate research and structure, and monetise attention through relevant advertising and affiliate partnerships. It can compound—but generic content will disappear into the noise. Original experience and trust are the edge.

Second: micro-SaaS. Find one annoying, repeated workflow and turn the smallest valuable part into a tool. Revenue can recur each month, but the real work is not just writing code. It is finding customers, earning retention and making the product genuinely useful.

Third: digital products. Package a clear shortcut into a template, toolkit, guide or prompt system and sell it directly. It is the fastest model to test, but easy to copy—so specificity, proof and distribution matter.

SignalFoundry helps you compare all three, test realistic assumptions and leave with a focused 30-day plan.

Use AI for speed. Keep judgment human. Pick one model—and build the signal.

## Short social cut (20 seconds)

Stop collecting AI business ideas. Pick one model you can actually sustain: useful niche content, a micro-SaaS that removes one repeated headache, or a digital product that delivers one clear outcome. SignalFoundry helps you choose, sanity-check the numbers and build your first 30 days. Pick a model. Build the signal.

## On-screen sequence

1. Cold open: “50 AI ideas. Zero direction.”

3. Show the model finder interaction.
4. Show the revenue sanity-check sliders.
5. Show the four-week roadmap switching between models.
